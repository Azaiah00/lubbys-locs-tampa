FAQ

Questions, answered

Straight answers from Lubby's Booksy policies and menu.

How do I book an appointment at Lubby's Locs?

Book online on Booksy, where you can see live availability. A $25 deposit is required to hold your appointment and is deducted from your total at checkout. You can also call or text (813) 520-1648.

I'm new. Which appointment should I book?

Book the New client appointment ($115, about 2 hours 15 minutes). It includes an introduction to Lubby's Locs, a complete consultation and recommendations, plus a wash, condition, palm-roll retwist and basic style. If you only want to talk through a plan first, book the $30 Consultation.

What are your hours?

The most accurate way to see open times is live availability on Booksy. For an appointment during closed hours, call or text; there is a $25 non-refundable convenience fee deposit for after-hours appointments.

How much is a retwist?

On Booksy, a Retwist locs box top is $70, a Wash + Retwist full head shoulders up is $90, mid back is $100, and Wash, retwist and two strand rope twist starts at $130.

What happens if I'm running late?

If you are 15 minutes or more late without texting or calling, you may be asked to reschedule and forfeit your deposit. A quick text helps.

Do you do kids' locs and haircuts?

Yes. Kids wash and retwist starts at $65, kids interlocking is $90, and the barber menu includes kid cuts from $15, teen cuts at $20 and men's cuts from $25.

Can you repair or reattach my locs?

Yes. Loc repair starts at $30 for up to 5 locs (full head $150), reattachments start at $30 for up to 5, and larger work like crochet retightening, combining and fillers is priced on Booksy and at consultation.

Is there parking and is the salon accessible?

Booksy lists parking, wheelchair access, Wi-Fi, a child-friendly space, credit card payments and a loyalty program at 1323 W Busch Blvd, Suite D.

Can I buy the hair butter or bonnets?

Yes. Hair Butter, Growth Oil, durags, bonnets and a loc stocking cap are on the Lubby's Locs shop page, with checkout on her secure Square store.
