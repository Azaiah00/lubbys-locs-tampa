FAQ

Class questions

Straight answers from Lubby's Booksy policies and menu.

How much is the loc class?

The 1 on 1 loc class is $500 for four hours, booked on Booksy. The loc styling lesson is $75 for one hour.

What does the 1 on 1 loc class cover?

Booksy lists starter locs, parting, maintenance, repairs, styling and products. It is a hands on class with Lubby.

What styles are in the styling lesson?

The one-hour loc styling lesson covers styles like fishtail braids, loc knot bobs and petal buns.

Is a deposit required for classes?

Lubby's Booksy policy requires a $25 deposit for appointments; it comes off your total at checkout.

Is the class for professionals or for my own locs?

Booksy describes it as a one on one, hands on class with Lubby. Call or text (813) 520-1648 to talk through what you want to learn before you book.
